# Data and method references

The experiment's own numerical sources are the JSON records in `results/` and the executed tables in `In_Hospital_Death.ipynb`. Historical notes are retained for context and are not independent publications.

1. PhysioNet. **Predicting Mortality of ICU Patients: The PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2012**, version 1.0.0. Dataset description, variables, original challenge partition and scoring context.

   ```text
   https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2012/1.0.0/
   ```

2. Silva I, Moody G, Scott DJ, Celi LA, Mark RG. **Predicting in-hospital mortality of ICU patients: The PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2012.** Challenge organisers' paper. The event-1 SAPS-I reference and best entry are contextual values from the challenge, not results on our re-split cohort.

   ```text
   https://physionet.org/files/challenge-2012/1.0.0/papers/0245.pdf
   ```

The course assessment requirement comes from the local `Group_Presentation/MD6117 Project Assessment Rubrics STUDENT.pdf`, pages 1, 2 and 5. This copyrighted assessment PDF is not duplicated into the public repository.

Earlier notes mention additional paper-level AUROC values without a recoverable bibliographic source. Those values are not used to claim superiority in the revised report or notebook. The GCS sedation interpretation remains a hypothesis rather than a cited or demonstrated patient-level mechanism.
